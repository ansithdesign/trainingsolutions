Five directions for the homepage

Same copy, courses, photos and structure in all five. Only the look and feel changes. Open any option as the full working page and flip between them with the switcher at the bottom.

My pick

Option 1 for the strongest identity. Option 3 if the client wants the safest, calmest read. I would not lead with 4 or 5 for compliance buyers.

1Layered Night 2Forest and Orange 3Airy Light 4Editorial 5Color Block
Option 1

Layered Night

A dark rounded hero with a collage of arch, circle and pill photos. Sections slide over each other like sheets as you scroll.

Palette

#0B1020 ink#2447F5 blue#FFB020 amber#F3F5FF pale

Feels

Confident and modern, like a premium learning platform. The photo shapes and rotating 1995 badge give it personality.

Trade-off

The most distinctive, and also the busiest hero. A dark opening reads more tech than HR, so it needs warm photography to balance it.

Option 2

Forest and Orange

Deep green and warm orange on peach. A centered headline over one wide framed photo, with an orange call to action.

Palette

#04372F forest#F8861E orange#FFF4EA peach#FFD9B3 photo tint

Feels

Warm, human and approachable. Photos take a soft sepia tint that sits well with the orange.

Trade-off

Friendliest of the five, but it leans toward consumer education. Less compliance gravity for buyers purchasing harassment and ethics training.

Open option 2
Option 3

Airy Light

White canvas, pale blue panels and light two-tone type. Flat sections, soft shadows and a single blue.

Palette

#000F23 ink#1D68D8 blue#F1F6FD pale#E6EEFA photo tint

Feels

Calm, credible and clean. The quietest option, with the most room for the content and the partner logos to breathe.

Trade-off

The safest read for HR buyers, and also the closest to a standard SaaS site. Least memorable of the five.

Open option 3
Option 4

Editorial

Monochrome with square corners, hairlines and one signal red. Large headline type, photos in black and white that turn to color on hover.

Palette

#0A0A0A ink#FF3B1D signal red#D92A0E deep red#F2F2F0 paper

Feels

Serious and premium, like a catalog or a ledger. Course codes and prices read as data, which suits how HR buyers scan.

Trade-off

The most designed look, and the least forgiving. It depends on strong photography, and the hard edges can feel severe for a people-focused brand.

Open option 4
Option 5

Color Block

Violet and lime in big rounded blocks. Photos are tinted lime, and each band changes color.

Palette

#5B3DF5 violet#D4F25C lime#14112B ink#EEEAFF lilac

Feels

Energetic and unmistakable. The lime photo tint ties every image to the palette.

Trade-off

The loudest and the most youthful. It would stand out in a sea of beige training sites, but it fits compliance training the least.
