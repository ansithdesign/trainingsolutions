Services, ten ways

The same six services each time, no cards. The tag on the right of each title says how much the photo matters in that idea. Try each one: hover, click, scroll, or use the arrow keys.

01

Index with a following thumbnail

Image: low

A plain typographic index. The photo only appears as a small thumbnail that follows your pointer, so the list stays scannable and the image is a reward, not the content.

02

One service at a time

Image: low

A single oversized statement with a counter and progress segments. Forces focus on one offer, and the photo shrinks to a small round inset.

03

Rows that wash with their photo

Image: texture

Thin type rows. On hover or tap the row floods with a navy-tinted photo and reveals the description. The image is atmosphere behind the words.

04

Scroll and the photo follows

Image: high

A large photo stays on the left while the list scrolls on the right. The picture changes with the service in view. Image-led, but the list still reads on its own.

05

A path through the offer

Image: none

Services ordered as a route, from running a class to certifying your own facilitators. It gives the section a story, and no photo is needed.

06

Spec sheet

Image: small

A comparison table with format, detail and price in columns. Best for buyers who scan and compare. A greyscale thumbnail colours on hover.

07

Start from what you need

Image: none

Visitors pick a goal and the matching services come forward while the rest fade back. Task-based, useful for people who do not know the product names.

08

Billboard with a selector strip

Image: dominant

One big photo with the title laid over it and a six-part strip underneath that doubles as a timer. Hover to pause. The most visual option.

09

Photo inside the type

Image: balanced

Large faded titles. The one you point at fills with its own photo, and its description sits alongside. The image is part of the typography.

10

Finder with a preview pane

Image: small

A search-and-select list with arrow-key support and a detail pane. Quick for repeat visitors and enterprise buyers who know what they want.
